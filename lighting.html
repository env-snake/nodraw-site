Lighting and sky preview

Development guide — public downloads are currently paused. See Changelog for release status.

Inspect lighting in the viewport and refine it in the background. Local edits reuse the existing bake and update affected surfaces. Sky preview includes the skybox, fog and sun direction. This is an editor preview: use a compiled BSP in the game to judge the final result.

Workflow

  1. Use viewport lighting preview to inspect a map without compiling a new BSP for every edit. Background refinement updates affected geometry after a local change.
  2. Rotate env_sun with the rotation gizmo to move the preview sun in painted skies. A separate 3D Sky layer supports editing, undo and conversion to a sky room.
  3. Preview timing depends on scene size, props and the graphics driver. Final colour, HDR, transparency and shadow parity are still under validation. Use a compiled BSP in the game as the final reference.
