Changelog

The current Windows test build is 0.1.0 beta 6. The changes below describe the included editing tools and fixes.

0.1.0 beta 6 hotfix — 23 November 2026

Download NoDraw — Windows x64
  • Fixed a crash when clicking the reset button on an entity property while editing its value. Pitch Yaw Roll resets to zero and supports Undo.
  • Split Polygon Brushes divides Polygon extrusions into brushes with three- or four-edge outlines while preserving volume, materials, UVs and groups.
  • Tie Each Brush to Entity creates one entity per selected world brush. The viewport context menu includes the func_detail_blocker class.

0.1.0 beta 5 — 23 November 2026

Editing and displacements

  • Undo selects the edited brush again. Paste Special accepts Enter, keeps entity links inside each copy and continues sequential names.
  • Arrow keys work during camera flight. A newly tied brush entity stays visible until deselected, so you can change its class in Properties.
  • Displacement tools keep grid and seam settings during gestures. Boundary pins, modifier previews and exact carving preserve the edited shape and vertex data through Undo, Redo and saving.
  • Model Viewer can turn supported meshes into textured brushes or displacements and rebuild static props. Propper retains paths and settings, splits high-vertex models into bodies and shares generated LODs with Model Viewer.
  • A trailing slash restricts material search to that folder. VMT toggles cover related reflection and Phong keys, and four-way displacement painting remembers its active layer.

Recovery, compilation and lighting

  • After a crash you can reopen an autosave and recover the saved camera and selection.
  • Compile Log opens the current map or areaportal leak. The file picker starts at the actual output directory. If graphics fail during compilation, the reopened editor can reconnect to the same run.
  • Lighting Preview filters direct brush shadows across lightmap cells and matching shared edges.

MCP

  • Browse brush entities by class or bounds, return PNGs in a batch and trace rays or sections against specific geometry types.
  • Read BSP leaves, areas, PVS and areaportal contours. Show the current leak line on a snapshot.
  • Preview a series of world-brush edits, review the outlines and apply the whole series in one Undo step.

0.1.0 beta 4 — 18 November 2026

18 November — documentation and demonstrations

  • Restored the NoDraw banner and the icons used in the editor interface.
  • Replaced the generic gallery with operation-specific demonstrations of Inset Region/Individual, Hollow, Bridge, Duplicate, X-Ray, displacement layers and Model Viewer.
  • Added compilation and MCP settings to their guides. Screenshots can be enlarged or opened at their original resolution.

18 November — current development

  • Fixed Model Viewer windows growing wider on their own; the layout can be resized and keeps controls within bounds.
  • Fixed a missing model after closing and reopening the same model; material editing no longer loses its GPU preview.
  • Model Viewer now combines preview, source controls, QC and build output, with icons, axes and cached LOD geometry statistics.
  • Verified the static-model QC/SMD → Blender → StudioMDL round trip with materials, visible physics, skins, LODs and an actual vertex edit.
  • Added reversible // switches for Phong, reflections and bump maps in VMT Text, preserving values and comments.
  • Added Face smoothing groups: smooth 1–24, hard-edge 25–32, mixed state, Clear, Undo/Redo and VMF persistence.
  • Brush entities open Properties by default; world brushes keep opening Tool.
  • Refreshed Welcome with the NoDraw banner; central Yes / No update prompts protect unsaved documents. Development builds no longer offer an older public package automatically.

17 November — stability and panels

  • Fixed the overlapping-patches save crash when different faces share an ID; material and UV batch edits retain distinct face targets.
  • Properties colour, reset, distance and model controls remain accessible in narrow panels.
  • VisGroups use the Scene outer scroll area, with Show all / Hide all and undoable user-group visibility.
  • Replaced the Pack tab icon with a custom NoDraw design.

Editing tools and navigation

  • Free rotation from the centre of the gizmo, axis rings, plane handles, uniform scaling and live handle feedback.
  • Ctrl+M is movable and resizable, retains the last mode and values, and previews transforms, Flip and pivot changes.
  • Duplicate previews rows, circles and spirals, retaining names, connections, grouping and one Undo step.
  • Lasso works for objects and Mesh components in 2D/3D with X-Ray, Shift/Ctrl and Escape.
  • Mesh Inset Region/Individual, inward/outward Hollow and segmented Bridge support live previews, Apply/Cancel and Undo.
  • Alt+Z toggles Mesh X-Ray; Alt+W toggles displacement wireframe. Arrow keys follow the current view; E in flight moves the camera instead of extruding.
  • Entity class lists support arrows and Enter. Updated Play and entity icons, and corrected live light-icon colour.
  • Restored displacement transforms and wireframe, added the Auto-sew control, and fixed Tab cursor capture during camera flight.

Assets, models and terrain

  • Fixed repeated double-click model replacement and asset-list position changes after clicking. Materials can be applied to selected brushes with a double-click.
  • Move to Floor supports complete-footprint seating, burial, slope strength, axis rotation, Ignore props, independent objects and preserved groups.
  • Colour picker previews changes immediately, restores on Cancel and commits one Undo on Apply; HSV/RGB/HEX and eyedropper are available.
  • Propper keeps source LODs, generates configurable LODs or builds LOD0 only; paths, materials, collision and placement are grouped in its controls.
  • Propper handles displacement and mixed geometry, four material blends, UVs, physics, source archiving and VMF Undo/Redo. Additional material effects remain under validation.
  • Road tools cover ribbon widths, blends, joins and crossings. 3D Sky editing supports a separate layer, undo, save and conversion to a sky room.
  • Corrected PHY preview axes, scale and rotation, and displacement edge shading. Animated-model collision preview remains open.

Rendering, compilation and distribution

  • Corrected sprite dimensions, Glow visibility, animation, blending, sun halo and overlapping sprite order. Transparency interactions, filtering in motion and HDR still need validation.
  • Painted sky sun follows env_sun rotation. Preview bake caching and local updates reduce repeated work; exact lighting speed and final game parity remain open.
  • CPU compilation is the default; GPU failures can retry lighting on CPU. The independent compile window has NoDraw styling, progress, Stop and Problems, and returns its log to the editor.
  • The per-user Windows installer supports upgrades and preserves the user profile. Beta 3 fixed HTTPS update checks and downloads; previous public installers are now withdrawn.

Still open or deferred

Lighting speed, full game-render parity, remaining transparency and material effects are ongoing work. Full Model/Mesh Editor, DispGen and Texture Editor are deferred; PCG expansion is outside the current stage. The experimental lighting replacement is not shipped.
