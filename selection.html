Selection, transforms and X-Ray

Development guide — public downloads are currently paused. See Changelog for release status.

Use a rectangle or Lasso in 2D and 3D. Mesh X-Ray lets selections pass through the edited object. The transform window retains the previous operation and values; Duplicate previews rows, circles and spirals. Move to Floor supports burial depth, slope alignment and prop filtering.

Workflow

  1. Use rectangle or Lasso selection in 2D or 3D. In Mesh, Alt+Z enables X-Ray to select components through the object.
  2. Drag an axis or plane handle to move or scale. Rotate has axis rings and a central free-rotation handle. Ctrl+M opens the movable Transform window and retains the previous mode and values.
  3. Arrow keys move Mesh components along the current view axes. In camera flight, E moves the camera upward and does not extrude a face.
  4. Move to Floor can seat the complete footprint, bury a model, align it to a slope, ignore props, and treat objects separately or preserve a group. Preview before Apply.
